Hommie Quiz 2027: Who Knows You Best?

A hommie quiz (also called a friendship quiz) is a quick game where you answer questions about yourself and your friends try to guess your answers.

How To Make Your Friendship Quiz

  1. Type your name and tap Start.
  2. Answer 10 questions about yourself.
  3. Save and share your quiz link.
  4. Share the link with your friends.

How Your Friends Play

Your friends open your quiz link, enter their names and answer the same questions. Their scores show how well they know you.

What Questions Look Like

Questions can cover your favourite colour, food, movie, music, biggest fear and dream destination.

Quick Tips

  1. Make the answer options interesting.
  2. Mix easy questions with difficult ones.
  3. Never share private information.
  4. Remember, it is just a fun game!

    5. Our Mission
